## house-style (live)

- **Cue:** an em dash in any written artifact; a head-noun-first name that breaks the
  `<qualifier>_<genus>` convention.
- **Failure:** the ship-slice scan red-gates it at rollup; catching it per slice is
  cheaper.
- **Fix:** commas, colons, periods, parentheses; glossary-conformant
  names.
