---
title: "A reachability probe runs on the node's probe interval, not every heartbeat"
date: 2026-10-28
status: "Accepted"
summary: "A node's reachability probes run on a probe interval of their own (`node run --probe-every`), at heartbeat granularity through the same per-task schedule as a driver poll; unset, a probe runs every tick as before. A learner's lab checks in every 2s and probes every 10s; a script's throwaway lab probes on every 2s check-in so a recorded walkthrough keeps its budget. A task is due within half a heartbeat of its due time, so an interval equal to the heartbeat never skips a jittered tick. Narrows ADR-0152's probes-every-tick clause"
refs: [1273, 1265, 822]

- **Date:** 2026-10-28 | **Status:** Accepted | **Pages:** [nodes](/architecture/nodes/), [collection](/architecture/collection/), [lab](/guides/lab/)
- **Decision:** (#1273.)
  1. **A probe interval of its own.** `node run --probe-every` sets how often each reachability probe runs. The node gates a probe task through the per-task schedule a driver poll already uses (ADR-0152): it runs on the first tick that reaches its due time (item 2), a probe new to the worklist runs at once, a probe whose endpoint's connection fingerprint moved (an edit in place, a rotated secret, an operator's `:retry`) runs at once as a driver poll does (#1061), and a late tick runs it once rather than catching up. Unset (`0`), a probe runs every tick, exactly as before, so nothing changes where it is not set. The ladder's informational ping runs with its probe and is skipped with it.
  2. **A task is due within half a heartbeat of its due time.** The schedule books a run from the time its tick was judged, after that tick's heartbeat publish and worklist pull, which vary by milliseconds; a tick that reached the task sooner than the one that booked it found it not yet due and skipped it, so an interval equal to the heartbeat skipped ticks at random (ticks 0, 2 and 4 of 6, at 8 to 15ms of jitter). The tolerance holds for driver polls too, which met the same skip whenever a `schedule.every` equalled the heartbeat. Each map the schedule keeps is swept by its own keys when a task leaves the worklist, so a task remembered but never booked (a probe at the heartbeat, a poll with no schedule) leaves nothing behind.
  3. **The lab checks in every 2s and probes every 10s.** The 2s heartbeat is the lab's on purpose: a change made in the console reaches the node within seconds. A probe on every 2s tick wrote most of a lab's rows, and a lab may run for days, so a learner's lab probes at a fifth of that rate.
  4. **A script's throwaway lab sets no probe interval,** so it probes on every 2s check-in. The demo pipeline's labs (`--ephemeral`) live for minutes, and a walkthrough that waits on a probe's verdict is recorded against the 60s budget (#884). Watching a rule raise an alarm runs 44 to 50s at 2s probes; a 10s probe adds up to 8s and would take it to the edge of the budget. A learner following that walkthrough waits up to 8s longer than its recording, and the lab guide says so.
  5. **Hosted instances probe at the product default.** The default heartbeat is 30s, so a node with neither flag set probes every 30s; a hosted chart sets the two numbers rather than a named profile. Either stays under the console's 150s staleness window, past which an endpoint only a probe watches reads stale between probes; making that window follow the probe interval is #1304.
- **Context:** #1273 began as named cadence profiles. Measuring the lab found that `--poll` is only the simulator's re-pull and that driver polls already honor their own `schedule.every`; the lab's row rate came from reachability probes, which ran on every heartbeat tick. Decoupling the probe from the heartbeat answers the profiles' question with one number, and a learner's lab writes about a fifth of its former probe rows (the probe metrics: `icmp-reachable`, `tcp-*`, `snmp-response-time`, `http-response-time`, `tls-*`).
- **Not chosen:**
  - **Named profiles** (`lab`, `default`, `hosted`). Sugar over two numbers no deployment yet needs; a later need can add them over the flags.
  - **A slower heartbeat for the lab.** It would slow every change reaching the node, the lab's reason for 2s.
  - **Probing every 10s in the recorder's lab too.** It would put the alarm walkthrough at risk of its budget on every recording.
