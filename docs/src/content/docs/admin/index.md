**For:** the people who configure Omniglass for everyone else: who can sign in and what they can
reach, what the fleet is made of, and what happens when something goes wrong.

**In this section:** users, roles, groups and grants; the audit trail; secrets and variables; the
catalog (vendors, products, drivers, standards, and the properties, metrics, events and commands they
define); and automation (rules, severity and notifications).

Until this section is written (#2158), the current pages are the **Admin guide** under **Being
rebuilt** in the sidebar, starting at its [overview](/guides/admin/).
