date: 2026-10-28

The `/templates` stub became a live Templates page inside the Catalog: the shipped catalog across its
four kinds, filtered by a `kind` facet that lives in the address. The Catalog's three Templates slots
(once soon, #615, #616, #617) open it filtered, each counting its own kinds of one shared list, and the
Systems slot reaches the standard templates too. A row's panel renders what the template creates from
the template body itself: a standard's roles and choices, a system's role fills, a place subtree as an
indented tree, a component's product and preset. **Start from this template**, gated by the create
permission of what it makes, opens the kind's form and posts the matching `:createFromTemplate`; a refusal
reads as the API words it. A success lands on the created row with `?from=`, where one shared notice
says the row is the operator's own and nothing follows the template. The standard, system, place and
component create flows carry **Start from a template**, carrying the place as the form's default. A
place is offered and posted by its dotted path. The stale "start a location type" hint is gone: a
location type is not a template kind.
