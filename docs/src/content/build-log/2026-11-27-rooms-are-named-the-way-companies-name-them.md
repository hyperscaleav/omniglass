---
title: "Rooms are named the way companies name them (#2757)"
date: 2026-11-27
refs: [2757, 2808]
---

A lab's systems were labelled by a generator ("Boring Edison") and its locations carried the address
("Hq Tower 12 01"), so the console read one room as an unrelated name on one surface and an address on
the next. The scenario now names each room the way a company does: one naming theme per site (the
headquarters after scientists, the regional and branch offices after peaks, rivers, parks, lakes and
bridges), a function for the rooms known by what they are (Huddle 3, Auditorium), deterministic per
room and unique within a site. The system's label is the name and the location stays the address. The
meeting and scheduling platform simulators list each room by its name with the address beside it, and
the console shows both everywhere a system is named (the system page, a device's systems, a conduit's
bindings, search, Explore and the system blade). The adjective-surname generator is gone.
