title: "The area labels match the labels page"
date: 2026-11-25
refs: [2645, 2028]
---

The live `area:*` labels and the labels page now list the same sixteen. `area:node` is renamed
`area:outpost`, the word the vocabulary uses (171 issues followed the rename). `area:web` folded into
`area:ui`, and `area:ai` into `area:assistant`; both old labels are deleted. `area:ingest`,
`area:assistant` and `area:ci` join the page's list.
