title: "Your first ten minutes is ten steps"
date: 2026-11-26
refs: [2762, 2156]
---

The first-ten-minutes page had grown into the lab's reference: readiness checks, the fleet's
collection methods, planted faults, the volumes and a list of every walkthrough. It is now the three
commands that bring a lab up and one ten-step example in the console (unplug a simulated panel,
watch the reachability alarm open, follow it to the system, clear the fault). What moved is on the
lab page (when it is ready, what the dev fleet holds, planted faults, stopping it) and the walkthrough
list is its own page, `/start/walkthroughs/`, which the lab's ready banner now points to.
