Thirteen `:::design` fences on the nodes, collection and variables pages still said their design
was tracked in #603, which closed with its follow-ups moved to #1187. Each was read against the code
and either re-pointed or retired. What the stateful arc built became built text: the server-baked
worklist and the secrets it carries to the node, the listen mode, the session lifecycle and where its
failures land, the extract rules every built transport shares. Two fences retired outright because
the design they hedged was superseded rather than pending: the `raw-tcp` table (the built `tcp`
transport, ADR-0153) and the config member's observed-side worker (ADR-0079 retired every maintained
current-value cache), and the provenance fence now says what ADR-0079 built, declared as the fourth
provenance.

What remains design names a live owner: the templates and `$var:` values (#827, #296), framing-driven
routing (#1057), the held transports ahead (#1036, #1039, #1040, #1041), snmp v3 (#1083). Where a
remnant had no open owner it got one, filed under #1187 where it is collection: a declared value's
reconcile policy (#1248), the node's component lanes, concurrent gate and verdict-gated polls
(#1249), secrets on a TLS bus decrypted on use (#1250), the raw bytes on a `collection-failed`
(#1251), the `name[instance]` suffix (#1252), the Expr transform and cascaded labels at the edge
(#1253), the session pool's knobs (#1254), and the cross-owner property cascade (#1255), the refile
the closed #291 asked for.
