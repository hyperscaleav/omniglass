---
title: "Troubleshoot an alarm with omnibot from its row"
date: 2026-11-18
refs: [2164]
---

Each active alarm row, in a component's alarms panel and in the Why strip of a component's or a
system's page, gains a bot button for a person who may ask omnibot. It opens the panel and runs the
triage-alarm skill on that alarm, handed by reference (its component and rule, or its component and
when it was raised for a hand-raised alarm), never by what the row shows. A cleared alarm offers no
button. The lab's recorded model answers the run (#2163), so the walkthrough plays the same way every
time.
