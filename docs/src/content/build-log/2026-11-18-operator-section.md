The operator guide becomes the Operator section, written for the people who watch and run the fleet.
A fresh overview names the two ways in (the console and the CLI), every page, how to get around and
what scope means for what you see. The entity page was doing three jobs, so alarms and health, and
commands, move onto pages of their own. Every capability page carries one status badge at its true
floor, and what is not built yet (dashboards, moving a component from its edit face, alarm flows and
storm rollup, the schedule hop, the closed loop's open refinements) sits in a design fence naming its
issue instead of an inline hedge. The old `/guides/operator/` addresses redirect to the new pages.
